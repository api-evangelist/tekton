We’re excited to announce that Pipelines-as-Code (PAC) is now an official Tekton project! After nearly four years under the openshift-pipelines organization where it was first created in April 2021, PAC has found its permanent home in the tektoncd organization at github.com/tektoncd/pipelines-as-code. GitHub automatically redirects requests from the old URL, so no action is needed. What Is Pipelines-as-Code? Pipelines-as-Code brings a Git-native workflow to Tekton.
